# Constraint Lab

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: React with Vite. The product is a polished, self-contained frontend prototype with synthetic scenario data and no required backend.

## Users

The primary audience is product-design hiring managers, design leads, product designers, product managers, and frontend engineers evaluating the creator's product judgment and light engineering ability. They arrive through a portfolio Playground and should understand the experience quickly without instruction.

## Product Purpose

Constraint Lab is a playable product-design experiment. Visitors complete a familiar digital task under a realistic interaction constraint, encounter friction, understand why it happened, and replay an improved version. Success means the visitor feels the effect of a design decision and can connect that moment to a concrete product improvement within a three-to-five-minute session.

## Positioning

Constraint Lab turns inclusive-design principles into a lived before-and-after interaction. It is neither a compliance scanner nor a generic AI audit: the user experiences the failure before receiving an evidence-linked explanation and improved replay.

## Operating Context

The first scenario is a synthetic checkout flow. The initial constraints are keyboard-only navigation, low visual access, and slow or unreliable connectivity. The experience appears as a standalone project inside a product designer's portfolio Playground.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One complete checkout challenge with three selectable constraints.
- A task briefing, interactive challenge, observable friction events, debrief, and improved replay.
- Synthetic product, customer, transaction, and performance data must be clearly labeled.
- AI may explain observed friction or generate implementation-ready recommendations, but it must not produce unsupported accessibility, medical, legal, or compliance claims.
- No user accounts, real payments, production integrations, collaboration, certification, or backend infrastructure in the initial version.
- The experience is desktop-first and responsive; core interactions must remain usable with a keyboard.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Constraint Lab. The product should feel serious, visually engaging, considered, and staff-level in its interaction and information design. It should avoid generic AI-product imagery and generic dashboard presentation.

## Evidence on Hand

No customer claims, benchmarks, testimonials, brand assets, or real product data are available. Demonstration content must be authored as synthetic material and never presented as external evidence.

## Product Principles

- Let visitors feel a constraint before explaining it.
- Tie every insight to observable interface behavior.
- Treat constraints respectfully and avoid theatrical simulations of disability.
- Make recovery and system feedback as important as task completion.
- Prefer one deeply authored challenge over a wide but shallow library.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The lab itself must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, semantic controls, clear state announcements, and sufficient contrast. Constraint modes are framed as design conditions, not as definitive simulations of a person's lived experience.
